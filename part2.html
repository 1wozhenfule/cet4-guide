第二部分　写作素材库

2.1 评分标准与阅卷逻辑#

写作满分 106.5 分（15 分制），30 分钟，120—180 词。阅卷是“先归档、再微调”：每份卷子先被归入一个分数档，然后在档内上下浮动一两分。所以最重要的不是“写得多漂亮”，而是“别掉出目标档”。

分数档典型特征
13—15 分切题，内容充实，语言准确且句式有变化，结构清晰，几乎没有语法错误
10—12 分切题，内容较充实，有少量语法错误但不影响理解，结构完整
7—9 分基本切题，内容单薄或重复，语法错误较多但能读懂
4—6 分部分偏题，语言错误多，影响理解，字数可能不足
1—3 分跑题、字数严重不足、大量中式英语无法理解

阅卷老师平均只用 30—60 秒看一篇。这 60 秒里的阅读顺序几乎是固定的：看字数 → 看分几段 → 读首段 → 扫每段第一句 → 看结尾 → 扫语言错误。所以首段、段首句、结尾这三处必须“零失误”。

12 分档的三条硬指标：

  1. 首段三句之内点题，且没有语法错误。第一句就出错，印象分直接下滑。
  2. 主体段有两个不同的理由，每个理由配一句解释或例子。两个理由说的是同一件事，等于只有一个。
  3. 结尾回扣题目关键词，不引入新观点、不写新的例子。

模板怎么用才不扣分：模板句控制在全文 20% 以内，只用于“过渡”和“框架”，观点句和例子必须自己写。阅卷老师对 "With the development of society" 这类句子极其敏感，一篇里出现两次就会被打上“套模板”的标记。

2.2 六类作文的万能框架#

2.2.1 现象解释型

题目让你描述一个现象并分析原因或影响。结构：现象 → 原因两条 → 评价/建议。

  • 首段：引出话题句 + 描述现象句 + 点明态度句。
  • 主体：原因一（个人层面）+ 一句解释；原因二（社会/技术层面）+ 一句解释。
  • 结尾：一句评价 + 一句建议。

骨架：Recent years have witnessed a sharp rise in ______. While it brings convenience, it also raises concerns. In my view, two factors are responsible for this trend.

2.2.2 观点选择型

题目给出两种对立看法，问你的观点。结构：分歧 → 立场 → 理由两条（含让步）→ 总结。

  • 首段：Opinions differ on whether ______. Some hold that ______, while others argue that ______. Personally, I side with the former/latter view.
  • 主体：理由一 + 例证；Admittedly, ______, but ______（让步+反驳）；理由二 + 例证。
  • 结尾：重申立场 + 一句建议或展望。

2.2.3 问题解决型

题目指出某个问题严重，要求分析并提出办法。结构：问题 → 危害 → 措施。

  • 首段：______ has become a pressing problem. If left unchecked, it will ______.
  • 主体：危害一、危害二（各一句）；措施一（政府/学校）、措施二（个人）。
  • 结尾：Only by ______ can we ______.

注意：措施要具体到“谁做什么”，不要写 "we should pay attention to it" 这种空话。

2.2.4 图表型

题目给柱状图、折线图或饼图。结构：总趋势 → 关键数据 → 原因/启示。

  • 首段：As is shown in the chart, the number of ______ rose steadily from ______ to ______. The most striking change is ______.
  • 主体：原因一 + 原因二（不要逐个数据罗列，只挑两三个关键数字）。
  • 结尾：预测或建议。

2.2.5 引言/谚语型

题目给一句名言，让你谈理解。结构：释义 → 例证 → 启示。

  • 首段：The saying "______" tells us that ______. In other words, ______.
  • 主体：一个具体例子（名人事例、社会现象、个人经历均可）+ 分析这个例子如何印证名言。
  • 结尾：联系大学生自身，写出行动建议。

2.2.6 书信与通知

书信有固定格式，格式分不能丢。

  • 称呼：Dear Professor Smith, / Dear Sir or Madam,
  • 首句：I am writing to ______（说明目的：apply for / express my gratitude for / complain about / invite you to）.
  • 主体：细节分条写，每条一句。
  • 结尾：I would appreciate it if you could ______. Looking forward to your reply.
  • 落款：Yours sincerely, Li Ming（题目给定名字就用题目给的）。

通知（Notice）另有一套格式：标题写 Notice 居中，正文说明活动内容、时间、地点、参加方式，最后留联系人，右下角落款。

  2. A growing number of young people are choosing to ______, a trend that has drawn wide attention.（越来越多的年轻人选择……，这一趋势引发广泛关注）
  3. The past decade has witnessed a dramatic rise in ______.（过去十年见证了……的急剧增长）
  4. Nothing is more visible in our daily life than ______.（日常生活中没有比……更明显的了）
  5. It is no exaggeration to say that ______ has reshaped the way we live and study.（毫不夸张地说，……重塑了我们的生活和学习方式）

数据类

  1. According to a recent survey, nearly 70% of college students ______.（据最近调查，近七成大学生……）
  2. Statistics show that the number of ______ has doubled over the past five years.（统计显示……的数量五年间翻了一番）
  3. A study conducted by ______ reveals that ______.（某项研究显示……）

问题类

  1. Despite the convenience it brings, ______ has also given rise to a series of problems.（尽管带来便利，……也引发了一系列问题）
  2. There is a growing concern that ______ may ______.（人们越来越担心……可能……）
  3. While ______ benefits us in many ways, its negative effects should not be ignored.（虽然……在很多方面有益，其负面影响不容忽视）

观点类

  1. When it comes to ______, people hold divergent views.（谈到……人们看法不一）
  2. Some argue that ______, while others insist that ______.（有人认为……也有人坚持……）
  3. Personally, I am convinced that ______.（就我个人而言，我确信……）
  4. I tend to agree with those who believe that ______.（我倾向于同意那些认为……的人）

引言类

  1. As the saying goes, "______." This proverb reveals a simple truth: ______.（正如谚语所说……它揭示了一个简单的道理）
  2. The remark that "______" reminds us of the importance of ______.（……这句话提醒我们……的重要性）

图表类

  1. As is shown in the chart, the number of ______ rose steadily from ______ to ______.（如图所示……的数量从……稳步上升到……）
  2. The chart reveals a striking contrast between ______ and ______.（图表揭示了……与……之间的鲜明对比）

书信类

  1. I am writing to express my sincere gratitude for ______ / to apply for the position of ______.（我写信是为了感谢……／申请……职位）

2.6 高分语法结构 30 个#

下面每一条都是“公式 + 例句”。背公式不如背例句，考试时改两个名词就能用。

1. 定语从句（which/that/who/whose/where）

公式：名词 + which/that + 谓语……

例：Online courses, which allow students to study at their own pace, have become popular.

2. 非限制性定语从句（, which …）

公式：主句 + , which + 谓语……（which 指代前面整件事）

例：Many students stay up late playing games, which does harm to their health.

3. 主语从句

公式：What + 主语 + 谓语 + is that … / It is … that …

例：What matters most is not how fast we learn but how well we understand.

4. 宾语从句

公式：动词 + that/wh- + 主谓

例：I believe that hard work will eventually pay off.

5. 表语从句

公式：The reason is that … / The problem is whether …

例：The reason why I support this plan is that it benefits both students and teachers.

6. 同位语从句

公式：抽象名词（fact/idea/news/belief）+ that + 完整句子

例：The fact that many graduates lack practical skills has aroused public concern.

7. 形式主语

公式：It is + adj. + (for sb) + to do / that …

例：It is necessary for universities to provide career guidance.

8. 形式宾语

公式：主语 + make/find/consider + it + adj. + to do

例：The Internet makes it possible for us to learn anywhere.

9. 强调句（It is … that …）

公式：It is + 被强调部分 + that + 其余部分

例：It is perseverance that makes the difference between success and failure.

10. What 引导的强调结构

公式：What + 主语 + 谓语 + is + 被强调部分

例：What we need most is a clear goal and steady effort.

11. 倒装：否定词提前

公式：Not only + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语, but … also …

例：Not only does exercise strengthen our body, but it also improves our mood.

12. 倒装：Only + 状语提前

公式：Only + 状语 + 助动词 + 主语 + 谓语

例：Only by working together can we solve this problem.

13. 倒装：虚拟条件句省略 if

公式：Had/Were/Should + 主语 + …, 主句

例：Had I prepared earlier, I would have passed the exam.

14. 现在分词作状语

公式：Doing …, 主句（主语与 doing 是主动关系）

例：Facing fierce competition, young people have to keep learning.

15. 过去分词作状语

公式：Done …, 主句（主语与 done 是被动关系）

例：Encouraged by his teacher, he decided to try again.

16. 不定式作目的状语

公式：主句 + to do / in order to do

例：Many students take part-time jobs to gain work experience.

17. 独立主格

公式：名词 + doing/done/adj., 主句

例：Time permitting, we will visit the museum after class.

18. with 复合结构

公式：with + 宾语 + doing/done/adj./介词短语

例：With the number of cars increasing, traffic jams have become common.

19. 虚拟语气：与现在事实相反

公式：If + 主语 + did/were, 主语 + would/could do

例：If I were the headmaster, I would reduce the burden on students.

20. 虚拟语气：与过去事实相反

公式：If + 主语 + had done, 主语 + would have done

例：If he had taken the advice, he would not have made such a mistake.

21. 虚拟语气：建议、要求、命令

公式：It is necessary/important that + 主语 + (should) do

例：It is important that every student (should) respect academic honesty.

22. 比较结构：as … as

例：Reading is as important as physical exercise.

23. 比较结构：the more … the more …

例：The more we practice, the more confident we become.

24. 倍数表达

公式：A is three times as large as B / three times larger than B

例：The number of online learners is three times as large as it was five years ago.

25. 被动语态

例：More attention should be paid to mental health.

26. 插入语

例：To be honest, I was deeply impressed by his courage.

27. 平行结构

公式：not only … but also … / either … or … / neither … nor …

例：A good student should be curious, diligent and open-minded.

28. 让步状语从句

例：No matter how difficult the task is, we should never give up.

29. 结果状语从句

例：He worked so hard that he was admitted to a top university.

30. 省略与替代

例：Some students prefer group work, while others do not.

使用提示：一篇 150 词的文章里，用 3—5 个高级结构就够了（比如一个定语从句、一个非谓语作状语、一个强调句或倒装、一个让步）。堆到八个以上，错误率会成倍上升，反而掉档。

2.12 四篇范文与点评#

范文一：现象解释型（短视频）

Recent years have witnessed a sharp rise in the number of college students who spend hours watching short videos. While these videos offer relaxation and quick information, they also raise concerns about time management.

Two factors account for this trend. First, short videos are designed to be instantly rewarding: a fifteen-second clip can make us laugh or teach us something new, which suits fragmented time perfectly. Second, peer influence plays a role. When roommates share funny clips, others naturally follow.

However, the cost is real. Many students stay up late scrolling, which harms both their eyesight and their academic performance. In my view, short videos are a double-edged sword. What matters is whether we can set a time limit and keep control of our attention.

点评：首段用 "has witnessed" 引出趋势，再用 while 句点出双面性，四十词内完成点题。主体两个原因分别落在“产品设计”和“同伴影响”，不重复。结尾 "double-edged sword" 用得恰当，最后一句给出可操作的建议，没有空喊口号。

范文二：观点选择型（网课能否取代课堂）

Opinions differ on whether online courses will replace traditional classrooms. Some hold that online learning is more flexible and cost-effective, while others argue that face-to-face teaching can never be replaced. Personally, I side with the latter view.

Admittedly, online courses have obvious advantages. They allow students to study anywhere and to replay difficult parts as often as they like. However, a classroom offers something a screen cannot: immediate feedback and genuine interaction. When a student looks confused, a teacher can slow down at once. Group discussions also teach us to listen, argue and cooperate.

Therefore, online courses are a useful supplement rather than a substitute. The best model is a combination of the two, with technology handling knowledge delivery and teachers focusing on guidance and inspiration.

点评：首段“分歧 + 立场”三句话完成。主体先让步再反驳，是观点型作文最稳的结构；"immediate feedback and genuine interaction" 用具体名词支撑观点，比空说 "more effective" 有力。结尾用 "rather than" 明确立场，并给出“结合”的方案，避免了非黑即白。

范文三：问题解决型（熬夜）

Staying up late has become a common habit among college students. According to a recent survey, more than half of them go to bed after midnight, and many do so simply to scroll through their phones.

The harm is easy to see. Lack of sleep weakens the immune system, reduces concentration in class and may even lead to anxiety. If left unchecked, the habit will follow students into their working lives, when the price becomes even higher.

To solve the problem, both universities and individuals should act. Schools can organize more daytime activities and offer lectures on sleep health, while students themselves should set a fixed bedtime and keep electronic devices out of reach. Only by taking sleep seriously can we stay healthy and energetic.

点评：开头用调查数据切入，具体可感。危害写了生理、学习、心理三个层面，但只用一句话排比，不啰嗦。措施分“学校”和“个人”两个主体，符合“具体到谁做什么”的要求；结尾用 only by 倒装收束，是安全的高分句式。

范文四：书信（邀请外教）

Dear Professor Smith,

I am writing to invite you to our annual Cultural Festival, which will be held on December 20th in the Students' Center.

The festival will include calligraphy, paper-cutting and a tea ceremony, all presented by our classmates. Since you have a deep interest in Chinese culture, we believe your presence would make the event more meaningful. You are also welcome to give a short talk about your own culture; ten minutes would be more than enough, and the audience would love it.

I would appreciate it if you could confirm your attendance by December 15th. Looking forward to your reply.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

点评：书信格式完整：称呼、目的、细节、期待、落款。第二段用 since 从句说明“为什么邀请您”，比单纯罗列活动更有诚意。结尾的 "I would appreciate it if you could..." 是书信万能礼貌句，注意从句用 could 而不是 can。
