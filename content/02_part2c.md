## 2.12 四篇范文与点评

### 范文一：现象解释型（短视频）

> Recent years have witnessed a sharp rise in the number of college students who spend hours watching short videos. While these videos offer relaxation and quick information, they also raise concerns about time management.
>
> Two factors account for this trend. First, short videos are designed to be instantly rewarding: a fifteen-second clip can make us laugh or teach us something new, which suits fragmented time perfectly. Second, peer influence plays a role. When roommates share funny clips, others naturally follow.
>
> However, the cost is real. Many students stay up late scrolling, which harms both their eyesight and their academic performance. In my view, short videos are a double-edged sword. What matters is whether we can set a time limit and keep control of our attention.

点评：首段用 "has witnessed" 引出趋势，再用 while 句点出双面性，四十词内完成点题。主体两个原因分别落在"产品设计"和"同伴影响"，不重复。结尾 "double-edged sword" 用得恰当，最后一句给出可操作的建议，没有空喊口号。

### 范文二：观点选择型（网课能否取代课堂）

> Opinions differ on whether online courses will replace traditional classrooms. Some hold that online learning is more flexible and cost-effective, while others argue that face-to-face teaching can never be replaced. Personally, I side with the latter view.
>
> Admittedly, online courses have obvious advantages. They allow students to study anywhere and to replay difficult parts as often as they like. However, a classroom offers something a screen cannot: immediate feedback and genuine interaction. When a student looks confused, a teacher can slow down at once. Group discussions also teach us to listen, argue and cooperate.
>
> Therefore, online courses are a useful supplement rather than a substitute. The best model is a combination of the two, with technology handling knowledge delivery and teachers focusing on guidance and inspiration.

点评：首段"分歧 + 立场"三句话完成。主体先让步再反驳，是观点型作文最稳的结构；"immediate feedback and genuine interaction" 用具体名词支撑观点，比空说 "more effective" 有力。结尾用 "rather than" 明确立场，并给出"结合"的方案，避免了非黑即白。

### 范文三：问题解决型（熬夜）

> Staying up late has become a common habit among college students. According to a recent survey, more than half of them go to bed after midnight, and many do so simply to scroll through their phones.
>
> The harm is easy to see. Lack of sleep weakens the immune system, reduces concentration in class and may even lead to anxiety. If left unchecked, the habit will follow students into their working lives, when the price becomes even higher.
>
> To solve the problem, both universities and individuals should act. Schools can organize more daytime activities and offer lectures on sleep health, while students themselves should set a fixed bedtime and keep electronic devices out of reach. Only by taking sleep seriously can we stay healthy and energetic.

点评：开头用调查数据切入，具体可感。危害写了生理、学习、心理三个层面，但只用一句话排比，不啰嗦。措施分"学校"和"个人"两个主体，符合"具体到谁做什么"的要求；结尾用 only by 倒装收束，是安全的高分句式。

### 范文四：书信（邀请外教）

> Dear Professor Smith,
>
> I am writing to invite you to our annual Cultural Festival, which will be held on December 20th in the Students' Center.
>
> The festival will include calligraphy, paper-cutting and a tea ceremony, all presented by our classmates. Since you have a deep interest in Chinese culture, we believe your presence would make the event more meaningful. You are also welcome to give a short talk about your own culture; ten minutes would be more than enough, and the audience would love it.
>
> I would appreciate it if you could confirm your attendance by December 15th. Looking forward to your reply.
>
> Yours sincerely,
> Li Ming

点评：书信格式完整：称呼、目的、细节、期待、落款。第二段用 since 从句说明"为什么邀请您"，比单纯罗列活动更有诚意。结尾的 "I would appreciate it if you could..." 是书信万能礼貌句，注意从句用 could 而不是 can。

## 2.13 中式英语 30 例（错 → 对）

这些错误几乎每次阅卷都会出现。写完作文，对照这张表扫一遍。

| 错误 | 正确 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I very like it. | I like it very much. | very 不能直接修饰动词 |
| There have many people. | There are many people. | there be 句型不用 have |
| I have been to Beijing last year. | I went to Beijing last year. | 明确过去时间用一般过去时 |
| He suggested me to go. | He suggested that I (should) go. | suggest 不接 sb to do |
| I am interesting in English. | I am interested in English. | -ing 修饰物，-ed 修饰人 |
| learn knowledge | acquire / gain knowledge | knowledge 不与 learn 搭配 |
| open the light | turn on the light | 开灯用 turn on |
| I very much agree. | I agree very much. / I couldn't agree more. | 语序 |
| according to me | in my opinion | according to 不接自己 |
| I think it is not good. | I don't think it is good. | 否定前移 |
| pay attention on | pay attention to | 固定搭配 |
| discuss about the problem | discuss the problem | discuss 及物 |
| marry with her | marry her | marry 及物 |
| contact with him | contact him / get in touch with him | contact 及物 |
| reach to the top | reach the top | reach 及物 |
| enter into the room | enter the room | enter 及物 |
| Despite of the rain | Despite the rain | despite 后不加 of |
| Although it was raining, but we went out. | Although it was raining, we went out. | 不能连用 but |
| Because he was ill, so he didn't come. | Because he was ill, he didn't come. | 不能连用 so |
| The price is expensive. | The price is high. / The item is expensive. | 价格用 high/low |
| My body is healthy. | I am healthy. / I am in good health. | 中文直译 |
| I will try my best to do it. | I will do my best. | try my best 冗余 |
| Can you borrow me your book? | Can you lend me your book? | borrow 是"借入" |
| In my opinion, I think ... | In my opinion, ... / I think ... | 语义重复 |
| As we all know that ... | As we all know, ... | 句式杂糅 |
| It is high time we do something. | It is high time we did something. | 虚拟语气用过去式 |
| The reason is because ... | The reason is that ... | because 与 the reason 重复 |
| I look forward to hear from you. | I look forward to hearing from you. | to 是介词 |
| There are many people think ... | Many people think ... | 一句一个谓语 |
| With the development of society, people's living level improves. | As society develops, people's living standards improve. | 搭配与句式 |
