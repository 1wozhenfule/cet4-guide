## 5.8 五段翻译练习与参考译文

### 练习一：茶文化

中国是茶的故乡。茶文化已有四千多年的历史。中国人不仅把茶当作一种饮料，还把它视为一种生活方式。如今，茶已经传播到世界各地，成为世界上最受欢迎的饮品之一。

参考译文：China is the home of tea. Tea culture has a history of more than 4,000 years. The Chinese regard tea not only as a drink but also as a way of life. Today, tea has spread to every corner of the world and become one of the most popular beverages.

难点：① "茶的故乡"用 the home of tea；② "把……当作"用 regard … as；③ "不仅……而且"用 not only … but also，注意 regard 的宾语位置；④ 最后一句用现在完成时。

### 练习二：高铁

中国的高速铁路发展迅速。截至 2025 年，中国高铁运营里程超过 4.5 万公里，居世界第一。高铁不仅缩短了城市之间的距离，也促进了经济发展。越来越多的外国人选择乘坐高铁游览中国。

参考译文：China's high-speed rail has developed rapidly. By 2025, the operating mileage of China's high-speed rail had exceeded 45,000 kilometers, ranking first in the world. High-speed rail has not only shortened the distance between cities but also promoted economic development. More and more foreigners choose to travel around China by high-speed train.

难点：① "截至"用 by，主句用过去完成时；② "居世界第一"用现在分词 ranking first 作结果状语；③ "乘坐高铁"用 by high-speed train。

### 练习三：春节

春节是中国最重要的传统节日，通常在农历正月初一。节日期间，家人团聚，吃团圆饭，贴春联，放鞭炮。孩子们最开心，因为他们可以收到红包。春节象征着新的开始和对美好生活的向往。

参考译文：The Spring Festival is China's most important traditional festival, usually falling on the first day of the first lunar month. During the festival, family members get together, have a reunion dinner, put up Spring Festival couplets and set off firecrackers. Children are the happiest because they can receive red envelopes. The Spring Festival symbolizes a new beginning and people's longing for a better life.

难点：① "通常在……"用现在分词 falling on 作伴随状语；② "贴春联、放鞭炮"是固定搭配 put up couplets、set off firecrackers；③ "对……的向往"用 longing for。

### 练习四：长城

长城是中国古代的伟大工程，全长约两万公里。它始建于两千多年前，主要用来抵御外敌。如今，长城已成为中国最具代表性的旅游景点之一，每年吸引数百万游客。1987 年，长城被列入世界遗产名录。

参考译文：The Great Wall is a great project of ancient China, with a total length of about 20,000 kilometers. It was first built more than 2,000 years ago, mainly to defend against foreign invaders. Today, the Great Wall has become one of China's most representative tourist attractions, attracting millions of visitors every year. In 1987, it was listed as a world heritage site.

难点：① "全长"用 with a total length of 作伴随状语；② "用来抵御"用不定式 to defend against；③ "被列入"用 be listed as。

### 练习五：移动支付

移动支付在中国非常普及。无论是在超市购物，还是在路边摊买早餐，人们只需用手机扫一扫就能完成付款。移动支付不仅方便快捷，还减少了现金的使用。它改变了中国人的生活方式，也吸引了全世界的关注。

参考译文：Mobile payment is very popular in China. Whether shopping in a supermarket or buying breakfast at a street stall, people can complete the payment simply by scanning a QR code with their phones. Mobile payment is not only convenient and fast but also reduces the use of cash. It has changed the way Chinese people live and attracted worldwide attention.

难点：① "无论……还是……"用 whether … or … 引导让步状语；② "扫一扫"意译为 scan a QR code；③ "减少了现金的使用"用 reduces the use of cash；④ "改变了……的生活方式"用 has changed the way … live。
