## 2.8 过渡词与常用副词

过渡词决定"结构分"。一篇 150 词的文章，用 5—7 个过渡词最合适：段首用 1 个，段内用 2—3 个。

| 功能 | 常用表达 | 使用提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 递进 | moreover, furthermore, in addition, besides, what is more | 不要连用两个，选一个 |
| 转折 | however, nevertheless, on the contrary, in contrast, whereas | however 后必须跟逗号 |
| 因果 | therefore, thus, consequently, as a result, hence | therefore 放句首时后面加逗号 |
| 举例 | for instance, for example, a case in point, such as, namely | such as 后面不接句子 |
| 总结 | in conclusion, to sum up, on the whole, in short, briefly | 结尾段首句用 |
| 顺序 | first of all, to begin with, next, finally, last but not least | 三个以上并列时用 |
| 让步 | admittedly, although, even though, despite, in spite of | despite 后接名词，不接句子 |
| 条件 | otherwise, in that case, as long as, provided that | otherwise 常放句中 |
| 强调 | indeed, in fact, above all, particularly, especially | in fact 常用于纠正前文 |
| 比较 | similarly, likewise, compared with, in the same way | compared with 后接名词 |

**数据表达**：写作里可以引用数据，但**不要编造精确数字**。安全的写法是模糊表达：

- According to a recent survey, a large proportion of college students ...
- The number of ______ has risen sharply over the past few years.
- ______ accounts for nearly one third of the total.
- The figure is roughly twice as large as it was five years ago.
- In recent years, the figure has more than doubled.

如果题目给了图表，就照抄图表里的数字；题目没给，就用 "a growing number of" "a large proportion of" 这类模糊表达，不要写 "73.6% of students"。

## 2.10 高分替换词表

作文想上 12 分，词汇必须"有变化"。但替换不是越难越好，而是"准确 + 不重复"。下表左边是大家都会写的词，右边是安全可用的升级版。

| 原词 | 可替换 | 提示 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| good | beneficial, positive, rewarding, constructive | 形容"有好处"用 beneficial |
| bad | harmful, negative, damaging, adverse | adverse 多用于影响、后果 |
| important | significant, crucial, vital, essential | crucial 语气最重 |
| many | numerous, a large number of, a wide range of | a wide range of 强调多样性 |
| more and more | an increasing number of, growing numbers of | 后接可数名词复数 |
| think | believe, hold, argue, maintain, be convinced that | hold the view that 很正式 |
| say | state, point out, note, remark | point out 强调"指出" |
| show | indicate, reveal, demonstrate, suggest | suggest 表示"表明"时不用虚拟 |
| get | obtain, acquire, gain, receive | acquire 多指技能、知识 |
| make | produce, create, generate, give rise to | give rise to 后接结果 |
| help | contribute to, facilitate, promote | facilitate 后接名词 |
| use | utilize, employ, apply | utilize 略正式，别滥用 |
| problem | issue, challenge, difficulty, obstacle | issue 最中性 |
| way | approach, method, means | approach to doing |
| thing | factor, aspect, element, matter | 按语境选 |
| people | individuals, the public, citizens, residents | 正式文用 individuals |
| very | extremely, highly, particularly, deeply | 修饰动词用 greatly |
| in my opinion | from my perspective, as far as I am concerned, personally | 别和 I think 连用 |
| I think | It seems to me that, I am convinced that, I would argue that | 多样化 |
| because | since, as, due to, owing to, in that | due to 后接名词 |
| but | however, yet, nevertheless, while | while 引导让步从句 |
| so | therefore, thus, consequently, as a result | 句首用 therefore |
| nowadays | in recent years, at present, in this era | 别每段都用 |
| a lot of | considerable, substantial, a great deal of | a great deal of 接不可数 |
| difficult | challenging, demanding, tough | demanding 指"要求高" |
| easy | simple, effortless, accessible | accessible 指"易获得" |
| famous | well-known, renowned, celebrated | renowned 更正式 |
| enough | sufficient, adequate | adequate 指"勉强够" |
| always | consistently, constantly, invariably | invariably 很正式 |
| never | under no circumstances, by no means | 放句首要倒装 |
